# Findings

## 1. Clear business impact

**Type:** product-defect
**Severity - triage:** MED
**Priority - triage:** normal

### Summary

Describe the customer-visible problem.

### Steps to recreate

1. State the role and route without credentials.
2. Give the minimum reproducing actions.

### Actual

Record observed behavior.

### Expected

Cite the agreed business rule.

### Impact

Explain who is blocked and what they cannot do.

### Screenshot / video

Attach a redacted still in findings/evidence; a clip is optional. None — template only; real findings require a still or an explicit reason.

### Other details

Include environment, exact code revision, case ID, dedup search and issue URL when one exists.
